About HackLearn Press

Last updated: 23 November 2026

HackLearn Press publishes practical cybersecurity study guides in PDF format. The books are written by Raj Thakur for students, analysts and career changers who want to learn by doing.

What makes the books different

  • Every technique is paired with how to detect and fix it.
  • Labs run on systems you own or a lab you build yourself.
  • Command outputs in the lab projects were captured from real runs.
  • Each book has a free 20-page preview so you can judge before you buy.

Who it is for

Beginners through to working professionals in network, web, cloud, SOC, malware analysis, Active Directory, red team and telecom security.

Our promise

All content is for education and authorised, lawful security work only. We do not sell tools that attack other people's systems, and we do not support unauthorised access.
