Delivery Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We sell digital products only. Nothing is shipped physically, so there are no shipping charges.

How you receive your books

After your payment is confirmed we send the PDF files to the WhatsApp number or email address you gave with your order.

Delivery time

Files are usually sent within a few hours and always within 24 hours of confirmed payment, during support hours (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 IST). Orders outside those hours are sent on the next working day.

If you do not receive your files

Check your spam folder and your WhatsApp message requests, then contact us with your payment reference at [Add support email] or +91 93411 27976. We will resend the files.

Digital delivery only

Because delivery is digital, shipping address, courier and tracking do not apply.
