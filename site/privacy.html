Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how HackLearn Press handles your information when you use hacklearnraj.in and buy our digital books.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you share to place an order: your email address, and your phone or WhatsApp number if you message us.
  • Order details: the books you chose, the amount and the payment reference.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, collected by standard website tools.

Payments

You pay us directly by UPI or by USDT (TRC20). We never see your UPI PIN, bank or card details. For UPI orders we keep the UTR reference and the payment screenshot you upload so we can verify the payment; for USDT orders we keep the transaction hash, which is public on the blockchain.

How we use it

  • To confirm your order and deliver the PDF files.
  • To give support and handle refunds.
  • To send important updates about your purchase. We send promotional messages only if you agree.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with authorities where the law requires.

Retention and security

We keep order records for as long as needed for support, accounts and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect them, but no online system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop messages, by contacting us on WhatsApp +91 93411 27976.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
