Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Our books are digital files delivered after payment. This policy explains when you can cancel or get a refund.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order before your download is unlocked by messaging us on WhatsApp +91 93411 27976. A cancelled order is refunded in full.

Refunds

Because digital files cannot be returned, we do not refund once the correct, working files have been delivered. We will replace or refund in these cases:

  • The file is damaged or will not open and we cannot fix it.
  • You received a different book from the one you ordered.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • We could not deliver the files within the delivery time.

Report the problem within 7 days of delivery with your payment reference.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. After we approve, your bank or payment provider usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.
